Who We Are

Established in 2022, SLN Consulting specializes in integrating and optimizing diverse systems and technologies to enhance operational efficiency.

The company was founded by industry veterans with extensive experience in business development, management consulting, technology integration, capacity building, and training.

SLN Consulting works with startups, commercial enterprises, multinational corporations, and academic institutions to build resilient operational capacity.
