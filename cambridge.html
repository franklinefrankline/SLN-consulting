Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this replace our academic curriculum?

No. It complements the institution's existing curriculum as an industry-exposure and career-readiness layer.

Is the program only theoretical?

No. It includes 400+ practical task scenarios, live mentor interactions, and technical capstone project enablement.

Does every student follow all seven pathways?

No. Students complete a common Month 01 Foundation, after which they are organized into a selected specialization pathway based on the institution's model.

Can the institution evaluate the internship?

Yes. The institution may apply its own academic evaluation criteria, with student progress reports and task evidence provided as mutually agreed.

Will this create significant additional workload?

The model is specifically designed to minimize coordination. Learning delivery, mentoring, assessments, and credentialing are fully handled by SLN Consulting.

Does the program guarantee employment?

No. The program focuses on verifiable practical skills, commercial cybersecurity exposure, and structured placement readiness.
